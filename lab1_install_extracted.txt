Introduction of Tableau  
Tableau [ˈtæbləʊ] is a company of interactive data visualization for bloggers, journalists, researchers, 
advocates, professors and students to make their storytelling expressive and insightful. It offers several 
main products: Tableau Desktop, Tableau Server, Tableau Online, Tableau Reader, Tableau Public, etc.  
Download Tableau Desktop  
  
Tableau offers a free student license (Fig.1).   
 
  
Go to https://www.tableau.com/academic/students, fill in the forms with your HKU student information 
to verify your student identity and download the software.

Another Option: Tableau Public  
  
If you don't want to download Tableau Desktop, you can use Tableau Public (Fig.3). Tableau Public has 
most of the features that Tableau Desktop has, all works created by Tableau Public must be saved on a 
public server, and when using Tableau Desktop, you can save your work on your local system.
